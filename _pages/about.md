I am an ALMA Project Postdoctoral Researcher at the National Astronomical Observatory of Japan and Nagoya University, working with Prof. Hideki Umehata. I received my Doctor of Science from Nagoya University in September 2026, supervised by Prof. Tsutomu T. Takeuchi.

My research focuses on galaxy formation and evolution. I study active galactic nuclei, infrared galaxies, galaxies at high redshift, galactic dynamics, and dark matter. My work also includes spectral energy distribution fitting and machine learning methods for astronomy.

## Beyond research

> What is the purpose of the Universe? <br> 宇宙的目的是什么？

is a profound question raised in one of my favorite short stories, *Epiphany (朝闻道, also known as "Heard It in the Morning")*, by the renowned Chinese science fiction author [Cixin Liu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liu_Cixin). Translated into English as "Epiphany". It was this ultimate question that genuinely astonished me and sparked my own journey to explore the universe's mysteries.
