My research examines galaxy formation and evolution through observations, galactic dynamics, and computational methods.

## Active galactic nuclei and distant galaxies

I study active galactic nuclei, infrared galaxies, and galaxies at high redshift. My recent work includes spectral energy distribution fitting, JWST observations, and studies of quasar environments and outflows.

## Galactic dynamics and dark matter

I use stellar kinematics to study the Milky Way's gravitational potential and compare dark matter models with alternative theories of gravity. My interests also include fuzzy dark matter and the relationship between dark matter and baryonic matter.

[Related paper: tests of dark matter haloes and MOND with Gaia DR2]({{ '/publication/2022-12-01-paper-1' | relative_url }})

## Computational methods for astronomy

I develop and apply machine learning methods for astronomical data analysis. My work includes the sOPTICS algorithm for identifying galaxy groups and clusters, as well as neural network emulators for spectral energy distribution fitting.
